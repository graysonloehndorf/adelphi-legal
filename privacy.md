# Privacy Policy

Adelphi is a personal AI assistant built and operated by Grayson Loehndorf. This policy explains what Adelphi collects and how it is used. Effective November 1, 2026.

## What we collect
- Your mobile phone number, if you opt in to Adelphi's text messages.
- The content of messages exchanged with Adelphi.
- Calendar and task information the account owner connects to Adelphi.

## How we use it
Only to send the reminders, schedule updates, and summaries you've requested, and to reply to your messages.

## Sharing
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run Adelphi, such as our SMS provider, and only to deliver its service.

## Opting out
Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
